Privacy notice

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This notice explains how JIKZO Creative handles information when you browse this website, send an enquiry, create an account or submit a project request.

JIKZO Creative is an independent business. This page is a practical website privacy notice, not legal advice. Please contact us if you have questions about how a particular item of information is used.

Information you provide

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, selected service and message.
  • Account details: the email address and authentication information managed by Supabase when you create or use an account. JIKZO Creative does not receive or store your account password in this website's own database.
  • Project requests: the selected service, your project brief and request status.
  • Reviews: the display name, rating and review text you choose to submit.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, discuss and manage requested work, provide account and request-tracking features, moderate reviews, protect the website and maintain its operation. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

This website uses Supabase for authentication and database services, GitHub Pages for hosting, and externally hosted images. When you use Google sign-in, Google handles its own authentication process. Their services are governed by their respective privacy notices and terms.

Sharing and public content

Enquiries and project briefs are intended for JIKZO Creative and are restricted by database access rules. Reviews are not displayed publicly unless approved. Do not include passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information in an enquiry or project brief.

Retention and your choices

Information may be retained while it is needed to respond to you, manage a project, keep business records or protect the service. To ask for access, correction or deletion of information associated with your account or enquiry, contact us using the details below. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business record-keeping.

Cookies and local storage

The authentication provider may use browser storage or similar technologies to maintain your signed-in session. This website does not intentionally use advertising trackers.

Children

This service is intended for general audiences and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission.
